Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

This policy explains how Sirus handles personal information across trysirus.com, the Sirus web and desktop applications, workspaces, automations, agents, integrations, and support services.

The short version. Sirus is a hosted, workspace-based service. It stores the business data you submit or connect so it can answer questions and run automations and agents. Relevant information may be sent to AI providers and connected services to perform the work you request. The desktop app can access local files and shell tools only when those tools are invoked, but selected content and tool output may pass through the hosted Service. We do not sell personal information or use Customer Content to train models owned by Sirus.

01 Who we are & our role

Sirus is a product operated by Parham Mohammad Ebrahimzadeh Sepasgozar, an individual sole trader (ABN 58 713 255 101) based in New South Wales, Australia ("Sirus", "we", "us", or "our"). Contact us at parham@trysirus.com.

For account administration, billing, website activity, support, service security, and our own communications, Sirus decides why and how personal information is handled and acts as a controller or equivalent responsible entity.

For personal information a business customer submits, connects, or generates in its workspace ("Customer Content"), the customer generally decides why it is processed and Sirus acts as its processor or service provider. The Business data processing section of our Terms governs that processing.

02 Scope

This policy applies to the Sirus website, web app, desktop app, hosted API, workspaces, workflows, agents, knowledge features, integrations, billing, and support. It does not govern a third-party service's independent handling of information after you connect or direct Sirus to use that service. Those providers have their own terms and privacy policies.

03 Information we collect

A. Account, profile & workspace information

  • Your name, email address, authentication records, login provider, and account status.
  • Workspace name, membership, role, invitations, and user preferences.
  • Security and session information, such as device identifiers, session tokens, sign-in records, IP address, browser or operating-system details, app version, and available desktop capabilities.

B. Customer Content

Depending on how you use Sirus, Customer Content can include:

  • chats, prompts, assistant responses, summaries, decisions, feedback, and attachments;
  • workflow and agent instructions, drafts, definitions, triggers, run history, step output, errors, approvals, and repair activity;
  • documents, files, records, messages, contacts, events, transactions, tasks, and other data imported or synced from connected services;
  • knowledge records about a business, including entities, people, organisations, dossiers, links, relationships, identifiers, observations, recommendations, and search embeddings;
  • content and output from tools, APIs, MCP servers, web search, and, in the desktop app, local filesystem or shell tools; and
  • credentials, tokens, and secrets required to operate connected services.

Customer Content may include personal or confidential information about employees, contractors, clients, prospects, suppliers, and other people. Customers control what they submit and connect.

C. Usage, billing & entitlement information

  • Plan, subscription status, billing period, allowances, credits, workflow runs, token usage, selected provider and model, estimated model cost, and usage source.
  • Stripe customer and subscription references and transaction status. Stripe processes payment-card details; Sirus does not receive your full card number.

D. Diagnostics, security & support

  • Service health, worker and scheduler status, device heartbeat, integration status, request route, response status, timestamps, and performance information.
  • Error messages, stack traces, failed step details, source or phase, app version, and technical context needed to investigate problems. We use automated scrubbing to reduce exposure of tokens, email addresses, local home-directory paths, and similar data, but diagnostic records can still contain Customer Content or personal information if it appears in an error.
  • Support messages, bug reports, product feedback, and communications with us.

E. Website and enquiry information

  • Email addresses and other information submitted through an enquiry or contact form.
  • Page views, approximate location, browser and device type, referring page, time on page, and scroll depth collected by the cookieless analytics described below.
  • Standard hosting and security logs, including IP address and request metadata.

04 How we collect it

We collect information:

  • directly from you when you create an account, communicate with us, configure a workspace, or use the Service;
  • from workspace owners or admins when they invite you or manage your role;
  • from services you connect and authorise, including through OAuth, API keys, MCP, or custom HTTP connections;
  • automatically from the app, browser, API, infrastructure, workflows, agents, and security systems; and
  • from payment, hosting, authentication, analytics, and support providers that help us operate the Service.

If you do not provide information required for an account, connection, or workflow, the relevant feature may not work.

05 How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure accounts and workspaces, apply permissions, and authenticate users;
  • provide chats, knowledge retrieval, automations, agents, integrations, local-tool coordination, workflow execution, self-healing, storage, and other requested features;
  • send instructions and relevant context to AI models, connected services, search providers, and other tools chosen for a task;
  • meter credits and workflow runs, administer subscriptions, process payments, and enforce plan limits;
  • operate, monitor, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the reliability and usability of the Service;
  • provide support and communicate about accounts, incidents, service changes, and enquiries;

  • understand aggregate website use. We do not use Customer Content for advertising or to train models owned by Sirus.

06 AI & automated processing

Sirus uses generative AI to interpret instructions, retrieve relevant knowledge, produce responses, create or modify workflows and agents, choose tool calls, and investigate workflow failures. To perform those tasks, Sirus sends prompts and relevant context to an AI model provider. Context may include connected emails, documents, customer records, tool output, or other Customer Content when relevant to the task.

Sirus uses third-party AI providers, including Anthropic, paid Google Gemini services, and DeepSeek. DeepSeek is used only when a workspace owner or authorised administrator selects it, which instructs Sirus to route relevant Customer Content to that provider. DeepSeek processes data in China and states that its services are not designed for sensitive personal data. Do not use DeepSeek for workflows containing that information. Each provider processes information under its applicable service and data-handling terms.

Automated actions. Workflows and agents may make automated choices and act in connected systems under the permissions and instructions configured by a workspace. Sirus is not designed to be the sole decision-maker for legal, employment, credit, housing, healthcare, insurance, or similarly significant decisions about individuals. Customers must provide appropriate human review and notices where required.

Sirus does not use Customer Content to train models owned by Sirus. Model providers' retention and training practices are governed by the service terms and settings applicable to our use of their APIs and may change over time.

07 Connected services & local tools

When you connect a service, Sirus stores or uses the credentials needed to access it and can receive, sync, change, or transmit information within the permissions granted. This may include email, calendars, files, messaging, CRM, accounting, commerce, project management, communications, and custom APIs. Disconnecting stops future access by Sirus but does not necessarily reverse completed actions or delete information already imported.

In the desktop app, local tools can list directories, read and write files, and run shell commands on the device when invoked. The desktop host performs the local operation. The file content, command, path, result, or error needed for the conversation may be sent to the hosted Sirus Service and an AI provider. Sirus does not continuously crawl the entire device merely because the desktop app is installed.

Web search is provided through a Sirus-hosted search service. Search queries may be sent to external search engines, and resulting pages are handled under those sites' privacy practices.

09 Storage & international transfers

The primary hosted application database, private object storage, and application compute are configured in Singapore. The web interface is delivered globally. Providers, support operations, logs, backups, connected services, and AI processing may operate in other countries.

We are based in Australia. Information may be transferred to Singapore, the United States, China, and countries where a customer-selected provider operates. Those countries may have different privacy laws and may permit government access under local law. We assess providers and use contractual, organisational, and technical safeguards where reasonably available and required. Customers with specific residency or restricted-transfer requirements should contact us before submitting covered data.

10 Workspace access & business data

Customer Content is logically scoped to a workspace. Account and workspace permissions determine which members can access or change features and data. Workspace owners and authorised admins can manage membership and may be able to access member profile information and activity within the workspace. Customers are responsible for assigning appropriate roles and removing access when it is no longer required.

For personal information about a customer's staff, clients, prospects, suppliers, or other people in Customer Content, the customer is responsible for its lawful basis, notices, consents, access decisions, retention instructions, and responses to those individuals. Sirus processes that information to provide the Service under the customer's instructions and the data-processing terms in our Terms.

11 Security

We use safeguards designed for the nature of the Service, including encrypted network transport, encryption at rest for stored connection secrets, workspace-scoped access controls, restricted database roles, authentication and session controls, private object storage, logging, and operational monitoring. Desktop credentials may also use operating-system protected storage.

No system is completely secure. Customers should use least-privilege connected accounts, multi-factor authentication where available, appropriate workspace roles, independent backups, and human review for important actions. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will investigate and notify affected parties and regulators where required, including under Australia's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme where it applies.

12 Retention & deletion

  • Active accounts and workspaces. We retain account information and Customer Content while needed to provide the Service and until deleted through available controls or a verified request.
  • Account closure. Closing an account may first deactivate workspace access to prevent accidental loss. On a verified deletion request, we delete or de-identify hosted Customer Content, ordinarily within 30 days, unless retention is required for law, billing, security, dispute resolution, or the rights of another workspace member.
  • Backups. Residual encrypted copies may remain in backups until overwritten in the ordinary backup cycle. They are not returned to active use except for disaster recovery or legal necessity.
  • Credentials. Connected-service secrets are retained while the connection is active and are deleted or made unusable when the connection or relevant workspace is deleted, subject to backup cycles.
  • Billing and legal records. We retain transaction, tax, accounting, and legal records for the period required by applicable law.
  • Diagnostics and security records. We retain logs, error reports, audit records, and abuse-prevention information for a reasonable period based on operational and security needs.
  • Website enquiries. We retain enquiry details until they are no longer needed for follow-up, or until a valid deletion request, subject to legal obligations.

Removing the desktop app does not by itself delete hosted workspace data. Files written to your device by a local tool remain on that device until you delete them.

15 Cookies, local storage & analytics

The website uses PostHog in a cookieless, memory-only configuration and Vercel Web Analytics to measure page views, approximate location, browser and device type, time on page, and scroll depth. Session replay and advertising tracking are disabled.

The web app uses essential browser storage or cookies for authentication, session continuity, security, device identification, and preferences. The app also sends operational heartbeats and diagnostic error reports so we can determine whether services and devices are available and investigate failures. We do not currently use app activity for advertising or cross-site tracking.

16 Children
